I am **Alex López-Cifuentes**, a Lead Research Scientist at [Xoople](https://xoople.com), where I work on computer vision and deep learning for **Earth observation**. I hold a PhD in computer vision from the [Universidad Autónoma de Madrid](https://www.uam.es), and I am based in Madrid, Spain.

My current research is about turning raw satellite imagery into representations that are actually useful downstream. That means **foundation models for Earth observation**, self-supervised embeddings over multi-modal satellite time series, and the practical question that follows from them: how stable and how transferable are those embeddings when you push them at real tasks such as land-cover classification? A recent piece of that work, [Temporal Sensitivity of TESSERA Embeddings for Land-Cover Classification](/publication/2026-tessera-temporal-sensitivity), was presented at the MORSE workshop at CVPR 2026.

Before Earth observation, I spent my doctorate at the [Video Processing and Understanding Lab (VPU)](http://www-vpu.eps.uam.es/) working on a single recurring idea: that **context** is an underused signal in visual recognition. That thread runs through [semantic-aware scene recognition](/publication/2020-semantic-aware-scene-recognition), [semantic-driven multi-camera pedestrian detection](/publication/2022-semantic-driven-multi-camera-pedestrian-detection), [attention-based knowledge distillation](/publication/2023-attention-knowledge-distillation-dct), and work on explaining what scene recognition models actually look at.

Research interests
======
* Foundation models and self-supervised representation learning for remote sensing
* Multi-modal and multi-temporal satellite imagery (Sentinel-1/2)
* Land-cover classification and Earth surface monitoring
* Scene understanding, semantic segmentation and contextual reasoning
* Model compression and knowledge distillation
* Explainability of deep visual models
