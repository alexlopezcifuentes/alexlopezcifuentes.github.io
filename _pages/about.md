I am **Alex López-Cifuentes**, a Lead Research Scientist at [Xoople](https://xoople.com), where I work on computer vision and deep learning for **Earth observation**. I hold a PhD in computer vision from the [Universidad Autónoma de Madrid](https://www.uam.es), and I am based in Madrid, Spain.

My current research is about turning raw satellite imagery into representations that are actually useful downstream. That means **foundation models for Earth observation**, self-supervised embeddings over multi-modal satellite time series, and the practical question that follows from them: how stable and how transferable are those embeddings when you push them at real tasks such as [land-cover classification](/publication/2026-tessera-temporal-sensitivity)?

A second thread turns the question around and points it at the research process itself: can an autonomous agent be put in charge of *discovering* Earth observation models, rather than just applying the ones we already have? [Evaluation of Agentic Autoresearch in an Earth Observation Benchmark](/publication/2026-agentic-autoresearch-eo-benchmark) is a first answer.

I came to Earth observation through industry rather than straight from academia. After finishing my PhD I was a senior computer vision engineer at Dive.tech, then Head of Research at Bronze, where I built the AI platform behind the company's computer vision products. I joined Xoople in 2025.

The doctorate itself, at the [Video Processing and Understanding Lab (VPU)](http://www-vpu.eps.uam.es/), circled a single recurring idea: that **context** is an underused signal in visual recognition. That thread runs through [semantic-aware scene recognition](/publication/2020-semantic-aware-scene-recognition), [semantic-driven multi-camera pedestrian detection](/publication/2022-semantic-driven-multi-camera-pedestrian-detection), [attention-based knowledge distillation](/publication/2023-attention-knowledge-distillation-dct), and work on explaining what scene recognition models actually look at. Before that I read the [IPCV Erasmus Mundus master's](https://ipcv.eu/) across Budapest, Bordeaux and Madrid.

Research interests
======
* Foundation models and self-supervised representation learning for remote sensing

* Land-cover classification and Earth surface monitoring
* Scene understanding, semantic segmentation and contextual reasoning
* Model compression and knowledge distillation
* Explainability of deep visual models
