Experience
======
* **Lead Research Scientist**, [Xoople](https://xoople.com) — Madrid, Spain (April 2026 – present)
  * Computer vision and deep learning for Earth observation
  * Foundation models and self-supervised embeddings over multi-modal satellite time series
  * Land-cover classification and Earth surface monitoring
* **Senior Computer Vision Data Scientist**, Xoople — Madrid, Spain (April 2025 – May 2026)
* **Head of Research (Computer Vision)**, Bronze — Madrid, Spain (April 2023 – April 2025)
  * Built the Bronze AI platform underpinning the company's computer vision products
* **Senior Computer Vision Engineer**, Dive.tech — Madrid, Spain (July 2022 – April 2023)
* **Pre-Doctoral Researcher in Computer Vision**, [Video Processing and Understanding Lab](http://www-vpu.eps.uam.es/), Universidad Autónoma de Madrid — Madrid, Spain (September 2017 – July 2022)
  * FPI predoctoral fellowship, Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities
  * Scene recognition, multi-camera pedestrian detection, knowledge distillation and explainability
* **Research Assistant in Computer Vision**, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid — Madrid, Spain (January 2017 – September 2017)

Education
======
* **PhD in Telecommunications Engineering** (Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Artificial Intelligence), Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, 2017–2022
  * Thesis: *Context-Driven Vision for Image and Video Analysis*, defended June 2022, **Cum Laude**
  * Advisors: Marcos Escudero-Viñolo, Jesús Bescós
* **MSc in Image Processing and Computer Vision (IPCV)**, Erasmus Mundus joint European master's, 2015–2017
  * Pázmány Péter Catholic University, Budapest — Engineering Information Technology (2015–2016)
  * Université de Bordeaux — Complex System Engineering (2016–2017)
  * Universidad Autónoma de Madrid — I2-TIC, Investigación y Desarrollo (2016–2017)
  * Thesis: *Online Contextual Updating in Multi-Camera Scenarios*
* **BSc in Telecommunication Technologies and Services Engineering**, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, 2011–2015
  * Specialisation in signal, audio and video processing, and computer vision
  * Thesis: *Identificación Automática de Materiales Usando el Sensor Kinect*

Skills
======
* **Research**: representation learning, self-supervised learning, foundation models, semantic segmentation, scene understanding, knowledge distillation, explainable AI
* **Earth observation**: Sentinel-1/2 time series, multi-modal remote sensing, land-cover classification
* **Engineering**: Python, PyTorch, C++, MATLAB, MLOps, distributed training (Slurm)
* **Languages**: Spanish (native), English (full professional, Cambridge CAE), French (elementary), German (elementary)
