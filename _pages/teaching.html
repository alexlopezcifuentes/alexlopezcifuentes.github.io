Alongside my research I teach regularly as a substitute lecturer at the Escuela Politécnica Superior of the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, standing in for permanent staff whenever a course needs covering. It has come round most academic years since 2024, and it is the same school where I read my own degree in Telecommunication Technologies and Services Engineering and later my PhD.

The courses have all been in signal, image and video processing. Most of my hours have gone into the laboratory and practical sessions, which is the part I enjoy most, because that is where students build the thing rather than only derive it.
