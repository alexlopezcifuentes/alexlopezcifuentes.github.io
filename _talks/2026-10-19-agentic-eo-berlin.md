Upcoming oral presentation at the [Agentic AI for Earth Observation Workshop](https://agentic-eo.berlin/), 19–21 October 2026, organised by the Berlin Institute for the Foundations of Learning and Data and the European Space Agency.

On putting an agentic loop in charge of discovering Earth observation models rather than merely applying them: autonomous agents that download, build, train and evaluate on the PANGAEA benchmark, and what they find when allowed to search either a fixed UNet recipe or the architecture itself.

Joint work with Juan Ignacio Bravo Pérez-Villar at Xoople.
