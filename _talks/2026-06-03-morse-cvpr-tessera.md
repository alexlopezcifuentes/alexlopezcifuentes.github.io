Extended abstract presented at the [MORSE workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/cvpr-morse/) (Multimodal Open-world Remote Sensing and Earth observation) at CVPR 2026, in the *Foundation Models, Vision-Language Models and Agents* track.

The talk covered how sensitive TESSERA embeddings are to the temporal sampling of the underlying Sentinel-1/2 time series when used as features for land-cover classification, and what that implies for practitioners building on top of Earth observation foundation models.

Joint work with Juan Ignacio Bravo Pérez-Villar and Julia Guerrero-Viu at Xoople.
