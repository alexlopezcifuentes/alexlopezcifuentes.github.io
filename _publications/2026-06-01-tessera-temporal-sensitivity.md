Extended abstract presented at the [MORSE workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/cvpr-morse/) (Multimodal Open-world Remote Sensing and Earth observation) at CVPR 2026, in the *Foundation Models, Vision-Language Models and Agents* track.

The work studies how sensitive [TESSERA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Feng_TESSERA_Temporal_Embeddings_of_Surface_Spectra_for_Earth_Representation_and_CVPR_2026_paper.html) embeddings are to the temporal sampling of the underlying Sentinel-1/2 time series when they are used as features for land-cover classification. This was Xoople's first scientific research output.
