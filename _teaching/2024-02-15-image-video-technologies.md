Substitute lecturer during the 2023 to 2024 academic year, from 15 February to 27 March 2024, teaching four hours a week.

Two courses in the degree in Telecommunication Technologies and Services Engineering. The theory of *Teoría de Tecnologías de Imágenes y Vídeo*, on how images and video are captured, represented, compressed and transmitted, and the laboratory sessions of *Televisión Digital*.
